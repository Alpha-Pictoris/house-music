Controls

Build
Drag a rectangle on the plot or tap its opposite corners. Draw adjoining rooms to extend the score.
Furnish
Choose a piece, then tap the floor. Moving it right plays its note later. Turn or retune a selected piece in the catalogue.
Listen
Press Play. Edits join on the next pass. Aligned pieces play together.
Keyboard
Focus the plot. Arrow keys move by a quarter unit. Hold Shift for a full unit. Enter places a corner or piece, R rotates, Delete removes and Space plays.
