# The bookcase and the mirror

*Playable discovery.*

Place a **Bookcase** from **Furnish → Living** in a room and select it. The first inscription can be read from the house view.

> Across the room, someone is trying on my face.

An open eye signs the reply, *It fits.* The letters in the reply run backwards.

“Across the room” draws the eye beyond the shelf to the furniture. The borrowed face and the backwards lettering suggest a reflection. The signature matches the eye in the standing mirror’s glass. Once you recognise the mirror, the reply gets worse. Has your reflection just found a face it wants to keep?

## The shelf hums

Put the **Mirror** from **Furnish → Bedroom** anywhere in the bookcase’s room. Either piece can face any direction. The writing changes even with the music paused.

> The shelf hums from left to right. Moonlight under cork, a single chime, the rustle of pages.

Choose **Arrange bookcase** on the note, or double-click or double-tap the bookcase. Fill the three spots on that one bookcase in the order the inscription gives.

| Spot | Clue | Object |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Left | Moonlight under cork | Moon bottle |
| Centre | A single chime | Handbell |
| Right | The rustle of pages | Little grimoire |

## Beneath the boards

The last inscription reads, “Beneath the boards, three golden fish are swimming.” Choose **Look at the floor**. A moonlit pool opens on a clear patch of floor, and a fish swims across the mirror’s glass.

Swap two objects and the water recedes. Undo the swap and it returns. The discovery follows the arrangement of the room, so a friend opening a share link to the finished house sees the fish too.
