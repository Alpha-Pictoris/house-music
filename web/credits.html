Sound credits

The instrument recordings use FluidR3 GM by Frank Wen and contributors. The basses were recorded at D2, the nylon and muted guitars at D3, and the electric piano, strings, harp and horns at D4. The distorted guitar plays a D power chord, the clavinet plays D3 and D4, and the upstroke guitar plays A3, D4 and A4. The drums include acoustic and electronic kicks, snare, closed hi-hat, side stick and handclap. The lead guitar, sliding choir vowel and talking synth start on D4. The baritone sax and layered group vowel start on D3. The low guitar plays a D2 power chord. The vocal parts use pitched soundfont samples, with pitch bends and vowel filtering for the solo lines.
