# The B-side

*Proposed discovery.*

## The loose shadow

Move a wardrobe beside a lamp. Its shadow lingers for a beat, then hurries after it. A corner of the ink curls up like loose wallpaper. Pull that corner and the shadow comes free.

The sweep would play the wardrobe’s part where it crosses the loose shadow. The wardrobe could sit against the wall where it looks good while its riff comes in two beats later. Drag the shadow back to the wardrobe’s feet to reunite them.

## Under the rug

Drag a loose shadow over a woven rug and it slips underneath. Something lifts the corner from below. Lift it the rest of the way and the house turns over like a record.

Downstairs, the player would recognise the odd-shaped rooms they drew upstairs. Moving shadows down here would compose a second passage. The two sides could take turns, one pass through the upstairs furniture and one through the downstairs shadows. A house with one furnished room could have a verse and a chorus. On the first changeover, someone downstairs might get up from a chair the player left empty upstairs.

A further discovery on this side of the house would lead to a defunct computer. [The cassette archive](cassette-archive.md)
