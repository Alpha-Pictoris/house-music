1. Catalogue

About the archive

The catalogue covers Portsmouth and Portsea Island. It includes dockyard plans, school and parish records, private letters, photographs and film.

City records

Portsmouth once kept its charters in a chest with the town seals. The records later passed through the gaol on Penny Street and the Guildhall, where they survived the bombing of 1941. The City Record Office opened in the rebuilt Guildhall in 1960.

In 2011 the Record Office and the Central Library’s Local Studies section became Portsmouth History Centre. History of the archive service, Portsmouth History Centre.
