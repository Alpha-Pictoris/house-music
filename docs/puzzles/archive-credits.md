# Archive credits

## Harbour print

*Harbor, Portsmouth, England* is photochrom print no. 10292, made around 1890–1900. The Library of Congress holds it in the *Views of the British Isles* collection and lists no known restrictions on publication. [Library of Congress catalogue entry](https://www.loc.gov/item/2002708061/)

## Cassette photograph

Tim Reckmann photographed the black cassette with the blue and white paper label on 3 February 2015. The archive uses a resized WebP copy with the house title and recording number added in ink.
