Noise complaint, Portsea. Filed 11 November 1998.

Letter from the lower flat

Manuscript letter, 10 November 1998. Transcribed in full.

Dear Sir,

Thank you for calling on Monday. I have put some of the music on tape as you asked. You can hear it best by the meter cupboard, even with the door shut.

Mrs Hall let me try her landing upstairs. It was quieter up there. I made the recording on my own landing when I came back down.

I should be glad of the tape back when you have finished with it. Mine is the blue sleeve.

Officer’s visit, 12 November

Inspected both landings with the tenant. Sound strongest at the lower meter cupboard. Requested section drawing BS/82/016 from Housing Maintenance.

Receipt for letter and tape filed under RO/98/012.
