Controls

Build
Drag a rectangle on the plot or tap its opposite corners. Start inside a room and draw beyond a wall to extend it. Start on the wall to build a separate room.
Furnish
Drag furniture from its tray into the room, or tap a piece and then the floor. Layer rugs underneath and plant the garden outside. Windows snap to outside walls. Double-tap or double-click a table or shelf to arrange small objects on it. Choose a spot, then an object. Pick an instrument to hear it play when the sweep passes.
Listen
Select a piece and open Fittings. Carvings, rings, studs, glass and strings each play a different instrument. Place one and press Play. With House band on, fittings play riffs over a shared bass line and drums. Moving a piece shifts its musical start to the nearest half beat. Switch House band off for exact floor timing. Edits to the house join on the next pass.
View and rotate
Drag with Pan to move the map or Orbit to turn it. Two fingers move and pinch the map in any tool. A middle-button drag pans with a mouse. Select furniture or choose a piece to place, then tap its curved arrow to turn it.
Keyboard
Focus the plot. Arrow keys move by a quarter unit. Hold Shift for a full unit. Enter places a corner or piece. With a table or shelf selected, Enter lets you arrange its objects. Comma turns furniture left, period or R turns it right. The bracket keys cycle through rooms, doors and furniture. Delete removes and Space plays.
